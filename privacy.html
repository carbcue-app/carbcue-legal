Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026
App: CarbCue (iOS)
Contact: kwabe.g@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what CarbCue collects, how we use it, and your choices. We wrote it in plain language. If something here conflicts with how the app actually works, please email us and we will correct it.

1. Who we are

CarbCue is operated by Gyasi Jackson (“we”, “us”). The contact email for privacy requests is kwabe.g@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

CarbCue helps you log meals and estimate nutrition from photos. Estimates are generated by sending meal photos to our backend, which forwards them to OpenAI’s vision models. Subscriptions are handled through Apple and Apphud. User data that needs to persist (such as logs tied to your anonymous account) is stored in Firebase.

3. Information we collect

  • Meal photos. When you take or choose a photo to analyze, that image is sent to our backend and then to OpenAI so the app can return nutrition estimates. Photos are used for that analysis. Do not photograph faces, documents, or anything that is not a meal if you want to keep those private.
  • Anonymous user ID. We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication. You are identified by a random Firebase user ID, not by your name or email, unless you later choose to contact us by email.
  • Nutrition and meal logs. Food entries, calorie/macro estimates, and related history you save in the app are stored in Firebase Realtime Database, associated with your anonymous user ID.
  • Subscription status. Purchase and entitlement status are managed by Apple (App Store / StoreKit) and Apphud so we can unlock paid features. We receive subscription status and related purchase metadata needed to verify access; Apple processes payment details, not us.
  • Technical data. Our hosting providers (for example Render for the API, Firebase for the database) may automatically process standard server logs such as IP address, timestamps, and device/app identifiers needed to run and secure the service.

We do not require you to create a named account with us to use the core scanning features. We do not knowingly collect your real name, home address, or government ID through the app.

4. How we use information

  • To analyze meal photos and return nutrition estimates.
  • To save and sync your logs under your anonymous user ID.
  • To manage subscriptions and paid access.
  • To operate, debug, and secure the app and backend.
  • To respond when you email us (for example deletion requests).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use meal photos to train our own advertising profiles. Third-party AI processing is described in section 5.

5. Who processes data for us

  • OpenAI. Meal photos (and related analysis prompts) are sent through our backend to OpenAI so vision models can return estimates. OpenAI processes that content under its API terms and data policies. Do not include sensitive non-meal content in photos.
  • Firebase (Google). Anonymous auth and Realtime Database storage for your user ID and logs.
  • Render (or similar host). Hosts our API that receives photos and talks to OpenAI.
  • Apple. App distribution, payments, and subscription billing.
  • Apphud. Subscription and paywall management using App Store purchase data.

These providers process data only as needed to provide their services. Their own privacy policies also apply to their processing.

6. Retention

  • Meal photos are transmitted for analysis. We do not intend to keep meal photos longer than needed to complete a request and operate the service. Provider logs may retain limited technical records for a short operational period.
  • Nutrition logs and anonymous IDs remain until you ask us to delete them, or until we shut down the service and delete associated data.
  • Subscription records are retained as required for Apple / Apphud billing and support.

7. Your choices and deletion

To request deletion of data associated with your CarbCue use, email kwabe.g@gmail.com from an address we can reply to, and include any details that help us find your anonymous account (for example approximate install date, or a user/device identifier if the app shows one). We will delete or anonymize Firebase data we control that is linked to that account, subject to data we must keep for legal, security, or billing reasons.

You can also stop using the app and remove it from your device. Uninstalling alone may not erase server-side logs; email us if you want those deleted.

For Apple subscription management or refunds, use your Apple ID / App Store settings; we cannot process card refunds ourselves.

8. Children

CarbCue is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Health disclaimer

CarbCue provides estimates only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Nutrition numbers from photo analysis can be wrong. Always consult a qualified health professional for medical or dietary decisions. Do not rely on CarbCue for managing disease, allergies, or medication-related diets.

10. Security

We use industry-standard hosting and transport encryption (HTTPS) for API traffic. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. Please avoid photographing sensitive personal information.

11. International processing

Our providers may process data in the United States or other countries. By using CarbCue you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

13. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: kwabe.g@gmail.com
